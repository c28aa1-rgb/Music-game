Match to Spotify

song-catalog.json ships with titles, artists and years but no track ids — inventing those would produce a pool that silently fails to play. This searches Spotify using the session signed in on this device and fills in the real ids and album art.

The catalogue year is the original release and stays unless you change it. Spotify's date is shown next to it for comparison, but it describes the version Spotify is serving — for older songs that is usually a remaster or a greatest-hits reissue, so a later date there normally means Spotify handed back a reissue rather than that the catalogue is wrong. Where Spotify is genuinely right, take its year with the button on that row.
